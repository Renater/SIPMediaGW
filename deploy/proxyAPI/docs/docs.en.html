SIPMediaGW: multi-platform room connector

Overview

The SIPMediaGW multi-platform room connector is an interoperability gateway that dynamically links meeting-room videoconferencing systems to a WebRTC-compatible videoconferencing platform such as:

  • Platform A
  • Platform B
  • Platform C
  • Platform D

A room system (SIP or compatible) can call the connector to join a videoconference, whatever its manufacturer or technical environment.

How to use the SIP room connectors

1

Create the meeting (required)

First, create your meeting on the target platform:

  • Platform A
  • Platform B
  • Platform C
  • Platform D

The room connector cannot create or start a conference.

Find the right conference identifier

The identifier to enter depends on the platform:

  • Platform A and Platform C → the conference name, i.e. the end of the conference URL.
  • Platform B → the invitation code given to participants.
  • Platform D → the full access URL of the conference, obtained from the platform management interface.

Once you have this identifier, you can generate the SIP address in the next step.

2

Generate the SIP address

From the service's address-generation interface, choose the target platform and enter the meeting identifier.

The service then generates a SIP address adapted to the conference you want to join.

Example: sip:[platform_number].[conference_number]@your-domain.example

3

Join the meeting from the room

Option A: call the meeting directly

  • sip:[platform_number].[conference_number]@service.example
  • or sip:[platform_number].[conference_number]@your-domain.example

Option B: call through the interactive menu

  1. Call: sip:9@your-domain.example
  2. Select the platform, then enter the conference number.

Option B bis: call through the interactive menu and QR code

  1. Call: sip:9@your-domain.example
  2. Scan the displayed QR code (or go to service.example), which opens a remote-control page allowing you to:
    • select the platform
    • enter the meeting name and the room display name
    • interact with the UI while the meeting is in progress

This option replaces step 2 (generating the SIP address): the meeting can be configured directly in the web interface or from the control page associated with the QR code.

4

Interact during the meeting

Once connected, press # to show the menu.

Available functions:

  • raise your hand
  • change the layout
  • show the chat
  • view the participants
5

Share content

Content sharing is supported through BFCP (presentation), usually exposed on room systems through an HDMI/VGA input.

Known limitations

  • A conference cannot be started through the connector
  • The meeting must exist and be active

Compatibility

Compatible with SIP equipment (RFC 3261), validated with:

Polycom Cisco Huawei Aver

Configuring your equipment

Enable the SIP protocol

To connect your equipment, set it to use the SIP protocol (RFC 3261) when it calls the room connector. Refer to the equipment's manual or to your videoconferencing integrator for this configuration.

Add the room connector to the equipment's address book

To make the SIPMediaGW room connectors easier to use, you can add the interactive menu number to your equipment's address book:

  • sip:9@your-domain.example
  • or sip:9@service.example if your equipment does not resolve domain names

Make sure media streams can be received

To communicate, a videoconferencing system must be correctly configured to receive audio and video streams. The exact setup depends on your network environment and your equipment.

  • Assign a public address to your equipment.
  • Set up explicit NAT between your equipment address and a public address.

Network best practices

To ensure video calls to the SIP room connectors work correctly, make sure your network and security policies allow the SIP traffic and media streams required by your environment.

Example configuration

  • Service SIP address: sip:9@your-domain.example
  • Public access address: 203.0.113.10
  • Service domain: service.example
